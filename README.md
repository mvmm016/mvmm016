# Mariana Miranda

**Statistics & Data Science Student | Bayesian Inference & Statistical Modeling | Co-founder of the Brazilian Statistics Olympiad**

Statistics and Data Science student at ICMC-USP (Institute of Mathematics and Computer Sciences, University of São Paulo). I like turning real problems into statistical models and seeing those models become decisions. My research applies Bayesian inference and Sequential Monte Carlo methods to locate wildfire sources with drones, and I co-founded the Brazilian Statistics Olympiad (OBE), where I work on financial data analysis.

## Experience

**Undergraduate Researcher — University of São Paulo** *(present)*
- **Advisor:** Prof. Glauco Augusto de Paula Caurin (EESC-USP)
- **Source Term Estimation:** Develop a Python simulation that combines Bayesian inference, a particle filter (Sequential Monte Carlo) and a Gaussian plume model to estimate the location and emission rate of wildfire smoke sources from drone measurements.
- **Experimental Study:** Ran 6 emission-rate scenarios with 50 random seeds each to measure how the signal strength affects the filter, identifying weight degeneracy as the main cause of error at high emission rates.
- **Dissemination:** Presented at SIICUSP 2026 and entered the Carolina Bori Science & Women Award.

**Co-founder & Data Scientist — Brazilian Statistics Olympiad (OBE)** *(present)*
- Co-founded a nationwide statistics olympiad for K-12 students in Brazil.
- **Financial Analysis:** Analyzed asset risk and return with linear regression models in Python and SQL, and built the organization's investment portfolio.
- **Backtesting:** Developed a backtest in Python to simulate and evaluate the portfolio's historical performance.
